FROM YOUR SENIORS

Notes for CEBS students

Everything about UM-DAE CEBS that never quite makes it into the prospectus — getting here, settling in, and figuring out how to actually enjoy it. Written by the batches before you, and meant to keep growing with yours.

01

Finding your way to campus

UM-DAE CEBS sits inside the University of Mumbai's Kalina campus (Vidyanagari, Santacruz East, Mumbai 400098), split across two areas — the Prefabricated Structures near the Anna Bhau Sathe Atithi Griha, and the Takshashila and Nalanda buildings.

  • Flying in: a cab or Uber from the airport takes about half an hour.
  • By train: LTT, Mumbai Central, CSMT, and Bandra Terminus are your main options — take a metered auto or an Uber from any of them.
  • Metro & local: the nearest metro station is Bandra Colony on the Aqua Line, which also connects to the airport. The nearest local station is Santacruz East.
  • Bus: route 318 stops on campus about once an hour.
ESSENTIAL APPS

Chalo for bus tickets, RailOne for local trains, and MumbaiOne for all the metro lines.

One more thing worth knowing early: everyone admitted in a given year becomes part of a Quanta — it's just how CEBS tracks batches. 2026 makes you Quanta 20, the twentieth batch of CEBS.

02

Hostel life

You'll be randomly assigned a hostel room based on availability, in either the Prefabs or Takshashila — girls are placed in Takshashila only. Take a stroll around the university campus early on; you'll struggle to find this much greenery anywhere else in Mumbai.

The basics

Quick-commerce apps are a lifeline here — consider them essential. For anything else, the Kalina market is a short walk from Gate 1. There's no dinner served in the mess on Sundays, so keep a nearby restaurant in mind for that one night.

Curfew

Sign the register if you're entering after 10 pm, and plan to leave the hostel before 10 pm. If you're stuck coming back late and something feels off, contact seniors — that's exactly what they're there for.

On campus

A music room, a gym, the mess, a table-tennis table, the library, a computer lab, sports equipment, and a volleyball court — all part of daily life here, not occasional treats.

MEDICAL PROTOCOL

If you need a doctor: The Health Centre near Gate 1 is the closest option, followed by Lilavati Hospital, Nanavati Hospital, and Cooper Hospital.

Beyond CEBS

University-wide facilities like the sports complex and Mumbai University's cultural events are open to you too. There's usually something on culturally — festival celebrations, get-togethers, and the occasional talk at TIFR or HBCSE worth crossing campus for.

Small batch, small world

Because CEBS batches are small, everyone is genuinely within reach — there's no real senior–junior complex here. Once meals wind down, the mess turns into the common room.

03

Academics at CEBS

CEBS keeps an institute-wide attendance rule of 85% — fall below it and it starts costing you marks.

"Attend all classes."

— Swagata Ma'am

If you notice you genuinely can't bring yourself to show up to class consistently — being unwell doesn't count against you — take that as an early sign something's off, and get help before it snowballs.

And if you actually are unwell: get a medical certificate from the university health centre or any doctor you trust, get it signed by the dean, and submit it to the academic office. That keeps your attendance protected.

Your first lab session usually comes with a proper lab tour. If your timetable allows, you're also free to sit in on classes from other batches. The 2.5-month vacation is usually when internships happen, so it's worth planning ahead for one.

The CGPA system

Grading runs out of 10 for each semester. Most students don't think much about the number itself — unless it falls below 6, in which case it's real: you lose your scholarship. If you spent school years prepping for competitive exams, that phase is over now. There's a lot more to explore and learn here than any single exam can capture.

Reports, LaTeX, and the research habit

Writing reports and research articles is core to life at a research institute, and everyone's encouraged to pick up LaTeX — it's the standard for producing clean PDFs with graphs, equations, formatting, even your CV. CEBS also sits inside a wider academic network — TIFR, HBCSE, BARC, IIT Bombay, and ACTREC are all nearby and worth knowing about.

Beyond the syllabus

This is a research college, so a rational, curious outlook helps everywhere. There's no formal channel for a good idea here — if you've got one, just reach out to the relevant club and get it done. Expanding your academic skill set is one of the best things you can do here. Talk to seniors, stay open to new ideas, and try to soak up as much as you can.

04

Mindset & the comparison trap

Most of you have spent your school years as the strongest maths, physics, or CS student in the room. Some students arrive here, see others further ahead in ability or knowledge, and get discouraged enough to stop trying, since they can't be the topper anymore.

That's a mistake. Being competitive is fine — useful, even — as long as it's pushing you to improve your own knowledge, skills, and grades. Some students take it somewhere more destructive: when they can't be the topper the way they used to be, their self-esteem takes the hit, they get demotivated, and their grades fall further still — which only makes things worse.

A HEALTHIER AIM

Try to be the best student you personally can be. Put in the work, take whatever help is on offer, and try to make your years at CEBS genuinely good ones.

05

Settling in

For a lot of you, this will be the first real stretch of time away from home. Missing it — the people, the routine, the comfort of the familiar — is a normal part of settling into somewhere new, not a sign that anything's wrong. It tends to ease as you build a life here, and CEBS's small batch size helps: you're rarely more than a conversation away from someone who's felt exactly the same way.

06

Asking for help, early

Talk to your favourite senior. Find out who's actually approachable at CEBS — among faculty, seniors, or the counsellor, if there is one.

Mental health is taken seriously here. If anything's bothering you, don't feel shy about approaching a senior or a faculty member — that's what they're there for. There's also a dedicated Women's Cell for matters that need specific assistance.

07

The bigger picture

What's the real scope of doing a degree at a place like CEBS?

Research institutes like this one work a little differently from engineering or medical colleges. Class sizes are small, and early on you spend your time learning the raw basics of science rather than applying existing knowledge to real-world problems. As you move up — bachelor's to PhD — your focus narrows and sharpens. It can get academically intense, but every college has its own version of intense.

At a premier institute, opportunities aren't scarce — internships, projects, and thesis work come your way both within and outside college, starting from your bachelor's or master's itself.

As for after: most of these institutes skip campus placements. Most people go on to a PhD, a few pivot into an MBA, and some head straight into industry or a government job. If you genuinely enjoy learning for its own sake, and you're fine taking your time to settle into life and career, this path suits you well.

— A R Bathri Narayan, Senior
